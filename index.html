Four-year roadmap

A simple plan you can adapt to your college and branch.

  1. Learn C or Python, build a base in maths, and get comfortable with Git and GitHub.

  2. Practise DSA daily, study core CS subjects, finish two small projects and join a tech club.

  3. Land an internship, build one strong AI/ML or web project, enter contests and prepare your resume.

  4. Take mock interviews, practise aptitude, revise core subjects and apply widely.
